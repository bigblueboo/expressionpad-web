## Design Context

### Users
Expression Pad is a continuous multi-touch instrument for musicians playing and shaping sounds on phones, tablets, and computers. It also controls external instruments through MIDI. Keep sound design and performance available across screen sizes.

### Brand Personality
Precise, tactile, exploratory. The creator requested a skeuomorphic Teenage Engineering K.O. II direction and identified the local Practicks project as a successful reference.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm grey enclosure, charcoal controls, restrained signal orange, inset olive-black readouts, raised keycaps, and fine printed legends. Carry over the material discipline and locally hosted IBM Plex typography of Practicks. The playing surface is the main event; instrument settings form expandable control banks.

### Design Principles
1. Put the playable surface first, including on small screens and in landscape.
2. Give controls convincing physical depth with consistent lighting and clear states.
3. Keep every layout, color palette, expression route, sampler, synth, effect, and MIDI control available.
4. Use real configuration in readouts and functional controls; avoid decorative meters and fake hardware.
5. Respect keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced motion, and existing saved configurations.
